About Samantha

Samantha Martinez is the President and CEO of StarFi Pay, a trade name of Starfighter Financial LLC, an Arizona limited liability company. She owns the company, runs it from Arizona, and signs for it.

Under Samantha, StarFi Pay is built around a few commitments: merchants see exactly what they pay before they switch, pricing is transparent interchange-plus, and every client has one dedicated point of contact instead of a ticket queue. The same approach carries across the company's three services — payment processing, payroll, and business insurance — so a business can work with one team for all three.

Samantha works directly with the merchants, referral partners, and sales team the company serves, and is the person accountable for how every account is handled. StarFi Pay serves businesses in all 50 states.
